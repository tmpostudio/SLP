Minor ailment prescribing in Penticton

21 minor ailments your Penticton pharmacist can treat

You don’t always need a doctor for everyday health problems. In B.C., pharmacists can assess and prescribe for 21 minor ailments, from UTIs and pink eye to cold sores and allergies. It’s free for B.C. residents when you’re seen in person at the pharmacy.

Good to know

Questions about seeing a pharmacist

Can a pharmacist prescribe medication in B.C.?

Yes. B.C. pharmacists can assess and prescribe for 21 minor ailments, including uncomplicated UTIs, pink eye, cold sores and allergies. You don’t need to see a doctor first.

Can a pharmacist treat a UTI?

Often, yes. A pharmacist can assess and treat an uncomplicated urinary tract infection. If you’re pregnant or male, or your UTI is complicated, you’ll need to see a physician or nurse practitioner.

How much does a minor ailment appointment cost?

The assessment is free for B.C. residents when it’s done in person at a pharmacy. If you’re prescribed medication, you may pay for it depending on your coverage, such as Fair PharmaCare.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring government-issued ID, like your BC Services Card, and your Personal Health Number (PHN).

What if a pharmacist can’t treat my symptoms?

Your pharmacist will tell you and point you to the right care, whether that’s a doctor, nurse practitioner or urgent care. Your visit might also end with self-care advice or an over-the-counter product instead of a prescription.

Is my visit private?

Yes. Pharmacists follow the same confidentiality rules as your family doctor, and we have a private consultation area for your appointment.

How do I book a minor ailment appointment?

Book online through the B.C. government’s booking site or call us at 250-422-4262. For help with online booking, call Service BC at 1-833-882-0022, Monday to Friday, 8 am to 4:30 pm.
