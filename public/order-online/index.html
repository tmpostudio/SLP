How to order online

Refills in a few taps

We use PharmaConnect, a free app that lets you order prescription refills any time. Set it up once, then it takes seconds.

Watch how PharmaConnect works

Ordering made simple

Five steps to your first online order

  1. 1

    Download the app

    Get the free PharmaConnect app from the App Store or Google Play.

  2. 2

    Get your connection code

    Call us at 250-422-4262 or stop by the pharmacy and we will set it up with you.

  3. 3

    Order your refills

    Pick your medications in the app and send your order in seconds.

  4. 4

    We let you know

    You will get an email or text as soon as your order is ready for pickup.

  5. 5

    Send new prescriptions

    Have a new prescription? Snap a photo and send it through the app.
