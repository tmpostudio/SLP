Our Story & Philosophy

Care rooted in community and personal connection

Born and raised in Penticton, BC, I have a longstanding connection to this community and the people who make it such a special place to call home. I attended school just down the road, and Skaha Lake was a familiar part of my childhood, making the opportunity to return and serve the community where I grew up especially rewarding.

I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Alberta before earning my pharmacy degree from the University of British Columbia. After returning to the Okanagan in 2014 to begin my career, I moved back to Penticton with my family in 2025.

Today, my wife and I are proud to be raising our two daughters here and contributing to the community that has long been an important part of my life.

Throughout my career, I became increasingly drawn to a model of pharmacy that allows for more time, more flexibility, and a stronger focus on the individual patient. While larger, high-volume pharmacies play an important role, their structure can make it challenging to provide the level of personal attention and continuity of care that I believe patients deserve. That realization is what ultimately led me to independent community pharmacy.

For me, independence means having the ability to make decisions based on what is best for the patient and the community. It means taking the time to listen, answer questions, understand each person’s circumstances, and build relationships that extend beyond a single prescription.

My goal is to create a pharmacy that reflects those values: personal, accessible, responsive, and deeply connected to Penticton. I want patients to feel known, supported, and confident that their care is always the priority.
